## 初始技能（0 转）（Zero Basics）

### Temple Recall（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Return to Zero's Temple.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 10, returns to Zero's Temple；Cooldown: 600 sec

### Dual Combat（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：A battle style unique to Alpha and Beta. Tags a Zero on standby, then moves slightly forward. Right-click to turn Forward Tag ON/OFF .
- **Lv.1**：[Tag] : While on standby, use Zero's skills to switch attackers.；Cooldown: 3 sec.；[Assist] : After using Tag to switch, for up to 3 seconds, use Assist for fire support. Cannot be activated while using the previous Assist skill.

### Divine Aura（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Channel the power of the Transcendents to increase the Attack Power, Magic ATT, Attack Speed, Speed, Jump, Defense, Elemental Resistance and Status Resistance of yourself and all nearby party members.
- **Lv.1**：Attack Power: +20, Magic ATT: +20, Attack Speed: +1, Speed: +20, Jump: +10, Defense: +500, Elemental Resistance: +10%, and Status Resistance: +10%

### Burst Jump（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Compresses time while jumping to extend your jump. Press the up key at the same time to move upward.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 5, while jumping, use the jump key to jump certain distance；Can be linked with Burst Step

### Burst Step（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Compress local time to move more quickly. Upward Burst Step can't be used immediately after using upward Burst Jump. Right-click to lock command.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 5, Press the arrow key and then use the skill to move in the corresponding direction.；Double-tap an arrow key to move in that direction.；Upward Burst Step can't be used immediately after using upward Burst Jump.；Can be used on a ladder or rope. Can be linked with Burst Jump.

### Rhinne's Protection（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Rhinne's protection increases your stats. Using the skill briefly makes Rhinne appear.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 10, the Goddess Rhinne appears.；[Passive Effect: All stats with a direct AP investment increase by 15%.]

### Resolution Time（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：5
- **描述**：Inherit the Goddess Rhinne's powers, adding them to your own. Strengthen this skill by collecting Goddess Teardrops.
- **Lv.1**：Permanently increases Final Damage by 5%, Critical Rate by 4%, Attack Speed by 1, Strength by 10, Max HP by 3%, and Max Speed by 4
- **Lv.5**：Permanently increases Final Damage by 17%, Critical Rate by 20%, Attack Speed by 1, Strength by 50, Max HP by 15%, and Max Speed by 20

### Rhinne's Blessing（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：6
- **描述**：You receive the Goddess Rhinne's blessings, which reduces the damage taken from enemies while ignoring a portion of their defense. Strengthen this skill by collecting Goddess Teardrops.  Lv. 6: Permanently increases Damage for Zero.
- **Lv.1**：Damage Taken: -3%, Ignore Defense: +2%
- **Lv.6**：Damage Taken: -20%, Ignore Defense: +15%, Damage: +4%

### Doubletime（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：When you use a skill, the duration is adjusted and the cooldown is reduced. A damage increase effect that can be stacked occurs depending on the attacker.
- **Lv.1**：Reduces Zero's attack skill cooldown by 4 seconds (excluding the used skill, Transcendent skills, 5th job skills, and some 6th job skills).；[Detect] : When Alpha attacks, damage increases by 1% for 20 seconds. Can stack up to 5 times.；[Strength] : When Beta attacks, damage increases by 1% for 20 seconds. Can stack up to 5 times；Effect for [Detect] and [Strength] remains even when tagged.

### Time Distortion（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Summon the power of the Transcendents to distort local time.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 30, Area of Time created for 30 sec.；[Enemies within the area] : Every 4 sec., buff cancels and stuns normal monsters；[Party members within the area] : Every 4 sec., nullifies magic and removes Abnormal Status, Attack Speed: +1, Damage +25%；You will receive same effect even when outside of the field, party only receives effect when inside the field.；Cooldown: 240 sec.

### Time Holding（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Manipulates time to reset your skill cooldowns. Grants invincibility and unlimited Time Force for a short time. Also increases damage for a short time if Zero is Level 200 or above. Time Holding is not affected by cooldown reset and does not affect other skills that are not affected by cooldown reset.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 10, invincible for 10 sec, reset cooldowns for all skills except those not affected by cooldown reset.；Increase damage by 10% for 60 seconds when Zero is Level 200 or above.；Cooldown: 120 sec

### Rewind（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Capture this moment in your Hourglass. If you get knocked out, time will rewind to the captured moment instead. Not affected by Cooldown reset.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 10, when you get knocked out you use up the buff instead, restoring all HP and becoming invincible for 2 seconds；Cooldown: 1800 sec

### The Transcendents' Will（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Hones your resolve to ignore certain abnormal status effects. Immune to most abnormal status effects for 3 sec. after use. Certain abnormal status effects will still get through. Does not apply Combat Orders. Can be used while using other skills.
- **Lv.1**：HP Cost: 30, Cooldown: 300 sec

### Shadow Rain（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Alpha and Beta's combination attack. Beta crashes down on the enemy with her Great Sword, stopping them in their tracks. Then Alpha follows up from the shadows, dealing critical damage to all targets.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 50,；[Beta] : Damage: 1600%, Max Enemies Hit: 15, Max Hits: 7；[Alpha] : Damage: 1600%, Max Enemies Hit: 15, Max Hits: 7；Cooldown: 300 sec

### Focused Time（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Increases the Attack Power and Magic ATT of all nearby characters using the power of the Transcendents.
- **Lv.1**：Time Force Cost: 10, increases Attack Power & Magic ATT by 4% for 2400 sec；Cooldown: 300 sec
