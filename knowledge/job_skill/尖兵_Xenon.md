## 初始技能（0 转）（Xenon Basics）

### Supply Surplus（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Recharges 1 unit of power at set intervals, and gives you a chance to recharge on hit or evade. Also increases stats based on your current level of charge. This skill may use up power. Android Heart can also be equipped in the Power Source slot.
- **Lv.1**：Recharges one power unit every 4 sec, and grants a 60% chance to recharge one unit on hit/evade. +1% all stats per extra power unit.

### Hybrid Logic（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：3
- **描述**：Activates your Hybrid Logic routines to permanently increase all stats.
- **Lv.1**：All Stats: +5%
- **Lv.3**：All Stats: +15%

### Multilateral I（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Grants a special bonus based on stats, once those stats reach a certain threshold.
- **Lv.1**：[At 20+ STR] 3% for Knockback Resistance；[At 20+ DEX] 3% for Elemental Resistance, 3 for Abnormal Status Resistance；[At 20+ LUK] 3% Avoidability；[At 20+ STR, DEX, and LUK] 3% Damage

### Promessa Escape（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Summons the Promessa to return to base.
- **Lv.1**：MP Cost: 30. Summons the Promessa to return you to base.；Cooldown: 600 sec

### Modal Shift（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Activates the Modal Shift system, allowing you to alter the attack mode of your skills. Use this skill again to change back.
- **Lv.1**：Use this skill simultaneously with another skill to activate its alternate modes. Reactivate this skill to revert back.

### Liberty Boosters（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Activate your internal boosters to fly for a short time. You can only use buff skills while airborne.
- **Lv.1**：Activate the booster to fly for 30 sec. Cooldown: 30 sec

### Mimic Protocol（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Lets you ignore equipment stat requirements. Also lets you equip Pirate and Thief equipment.
- **Lv.1**：Lets you ignore equipment stat requirements. Also lets you equip Pirate and Thief equipment.

### Star Force Conversion（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Empower yourself by harnessing the star power of your equipment. Increases STR, DEX, and LUK by 7 for every 10 Star Force provided by your equipment (up to a limit of 100 Star Force). Star Force Enhancements on Medals and Titles do not contribute to Star Force Conversion, nor do Star Force bonuses from guild skills.
- **Lv.1**：Increases STR, DEX and LUK in proportion to the Star Force of your equipped items

### Hero's Echo（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：[Master Level: 1] Increases the Attack Power and Magic ATT of all nearby characters. Cooldown: 300 sec
- **Lv.1**：MP Cost: 30, Attack Power and Magic ATT: +4%, Duration: 40 min
