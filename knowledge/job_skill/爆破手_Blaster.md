- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Use your Arm Cannon to create additional explosions and deal damage. This cannot be used as a standalone skill. - **Lv.1**：Uses 1 Ammo, and your Ammo is reloaded if used as a key-down skill

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：10
- **描述**：Depletes the Dynamo Gauge to fire your Bunker Buster, releasing explosions and shockwaves. Ignores Defense Rate of enemies in melee range. Can be linked into Hammer Smash. Unaffected by attack reflection. Required Skill: Revolving Cannon Mastery Lv. 5+

While Overheated, the Dynamo Gauge is set to max, and you cannot charge the Dynamo Gauge or use Bunker Buster Explosion. On ending, depletes the Dynamo Gauge.
- **Lv.10**：Requires 3 or more Dynamo Gauge bars. Attack up to 10 enemies for 100% damage 8 times. Melee hits ignore 80% of enemy Defense Rate.；Releases Shockwave 1, attacking up to 10 enemies for 50% damage 6 times.；After use, you will Overheat for 7 sec. While Overheated, the Dynamo Gauge is set to max, and you cannot charge the Dynamo Gauge or use Bunker Buster Explosion. On ending, depletes the Dynamo Gauge.
