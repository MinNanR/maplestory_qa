## 初始技能（0 转）（Light Control）

### Sunfire（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Fill your light gauge completely to gain bonus damage with light magic. Each light magic attack will also restore some HP.
- **Lv.1**：Light skills deal an additional hit dealing 50% of Final Damage and restoring 1% of your base HP per cast.

### Eclipse（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Fill your dark gauge completely to gain bonus damage with dark magic. Dark magic uses 0 MP. Character Appearance Change On/Off: Right-click
- **Lv.1**：Dark skills deal an additional hit proportional to the number of attacks that is 50% of final damage and use 0 MP.

### Light Wash（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：3
- **描述**：The power of light breaks down enemy DEF, allowing your attacks to penetrate.  Level 3: Permanently increases Damage for Luminous.
- **Lv.1**：Ignore Defense: +10%
- **Lv.3**：Ignore Defense: +20%, Damage: +4%

### Equilibrium（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Your perfect balance between light and dark increases Knockback Resistance and reduces all enemy attacks to 1 damage. All your spells will deal extra damage with no cooldown. Light spells will recover HP, and dark spells will not use MP. Character Appearance Change on/off: Right-click
- **Lv.1**：Knockback Resistance +100% for 20 sec.；When using light and dark spells, delivers an additional attack inflicting 50% of Final Damage for each hit.；When Equilibrium is active, delivers an additional attack inflicting 100% of Final Damage for each hit. Ignores all cooldowns.；Dark spells cost 0 MP. Light spells restore 1% of HP.；The total number of hits cannot exceed 15 for both Light and Dark attacks.

### Inner Light（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Increases Willpower, Insight, and Intelligence. The power of light deflects all Darkness.
- **Lv.1**：Willpower Lv. 20, Insight Lv. 20, INT +20 , more MP increased from leveling, permanent immunity to the Darkness status.

### Flash Blink（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Transform into light and teleport to a random location on the field.
- **Lv.1**：MP Cost: 40. Teleport to a random location on the field.

### Hero's Echo（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：[Master Level: 1] Increases the Attack Power and Magic ATT of all nearby characters. Cooldown: 300 sec
- **Lv.1**：MP Cost: 30, Attack Power and Magic ATT: +4%, Duration: 40 min
