## 1 转技能（The Archer's Art）

### Curse Dampening I（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Releases power built up in the relic through continuous combat to weaken the effects of the Ancient Curse.

### Cardinal Deluge（Active）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：20
- **描述**：[Cardinal Force] An archery technique that zeroes in on targets within range. Guided shots target the boss with the highest HP first if there is a boss within range, and are unaffected by attack reflection.

- **Lv.20**：MP Cost: 10, Max Enemies Hit: 4. Blasts an arrow that deals 90% damage 4 times.

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：10

### Relic Charge I（Passive）

- **精通等级 (Master Level)**：1
- **描述**：Gathers energy in the relic through combat. The use of a Cardinal Force skill will cause the relic's emblem to shift, and a successful hit will add to the gauge.
